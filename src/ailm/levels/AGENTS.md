# AGENTS.md — src/ailm/levels/

The highest-risk directory in the repository. Every defect this project has shipped
originated here or was measured here.

## Ownership boundary

`levels/` owns the generative models and their metrics: matrix builders, `Agent`
construction, perplexity, decoding, and the context-free comparators those metrics
are judged against. It does **not** own:

- the numbers those metrics produce → `data/claims.json`, written by
  `scripts/record_stage*_claims.py`
- the independent check on those numbers → `ailm/verification/oracle.py`
- whether a threshold is meaningful → `ailm/control.py` and `scripts/audit_gates.py`
- the vendored artifacts it reads → `vendor/`, regenerated by `scripts/`

A module here may read `vendor/*.json`. It may not write anything, print anything,
parse argv, or touch the network.

## May change freely

- Add a new level module (`word.py`, `case.py`, …) with its own gate test in
  `tests/`, a positive and a negative control, and an `audit_*` entry in
  `scripts/audit_gates.py` moving that stage out of `UNAUDITED_STAGES`.
- Add a comparator or a diagnostic that makes an existing gate stricter.
- Extend a docstring. The docstrings in `letter.py` and `phoneme.py` are the
  project's primary design record; `phoneme.py`'s in particular works through the
  float32 tolerance, the two circulating bigram numbers, and why they are not
  interchangeable. Do not compress them.

## Must not change without an explicit decision

**Do not move learned statistics from `B` into `A`.** Sequence dynamics are the
transition; the emission is `A`. Reversing them yields a posterior over the
*preceding* symbol, and `pymdp._validate()` will accept it because a bigram matrix is
column-stochastic in either slot. Measured cost at Level 1:
claim:stage1_anti_informative_perplexity against claim:stage1_letter_perplexity.
`tests/test_stage1_gate.py` keeps a live negative control against the mis-wiring;
`tests/test_phoneme.py` and `tests/test_stage2_gate.py` do the same at Level 2 and
additionally re-derive `A[l, p] = P(letter l | phone p)` in independent numpy.

**Do not weaken the emission's column-sum invariant.** The `A`-normalized-over-phones
mis-wiring *beat the recorded baseline while being wrong* — the control retained in
`tests/test_stage2_gate.py` and tabulated in the manuscript's mis-wiring signatures —
so an accuracy comparison alone cannot detect it. The invariant is
`A[0, :, p]` sums to 1 *before* any renormalization. Renormalizing a mis-normalized
matrix to satisfy pymdp launders the defect.

**Do not turn `_iter_pairs` into a prefix, and do not "simplify" the index
arithmetic.** `[pairs[i * len(pairs) // limit] for i in range(limit)]` is deliberate:
a plain `pairs[::stride][:limit]` spans the range and then truncates the tail back
off, which for the full test split (claim:stage2_g2p_test_pairs words) at `limit=1200` silently drops every word from "u" onward.
A prefix of the alphabetically sorted list covers only a/b/c words and inflated a
position-aligned letter-to-phone (G2P) margin
claim:subsample_prefix_overstatement_factor× (+claim:subsample_prefix_margin_pts pts against the true claim:stage2_g2p_margin_pts pts).

**Do not remove the per-sequence prior reset** in `compute_phoneme_perplexity`, and
do not concatenate sequences. `tests/test_phoneme.py` pins both
(`test_sequences_are_independent_not_concatenated`).

**Do not add smoothing, change a smoothing constant, or switch `float32`/`float64`
without re-recording every affected claim.** Add-1 Laplace is assumed by the recorded
floors, and the two Part A numbers that circulate are not interchangeable: the
all-positions add-1-smoothed perplexity (claim:stage2_phoneme_perplexity) versus the
unsmoothed transitions-only conditional-entropy floor
(claim:stage2_phoneme_floor_perplexity). A gate asserting the first equals the second
is unsatisfiable by construction. The comparable gate bound is the unsmoothed
start-plus-transition MLE over the identical denominator,
claim:stage2_phoneme_all_position_floor_perplexity.

**Do not gate against uniform.** Uniform over the inventory
(claim:stage1_uniform_baseline letters, claim:stage2_phoneme_uniform_baseline phones)
is a crippled comparator that a context-free frequency table already clears. The
honest bars are claim:stage1_unigram_baseline,
claim:stage2_phoneme_unigram_baseline, and
claim:stage2_g2p_baseline_accuracy. Equally, do not port Stage 1's original
"≥2 nats over uniform" wording forward — the conditional entropy of the training
distribution caps the achievable gain at claim:stage1_max_bigram_gain_nats nats and
the phone level at claim:stage2_phoneme_max_gain_nats, so that target is
*unreachable* at order 1 regardless of implementation quality.

**Do not add a stage's model before auditing its gate.** The original Stage 3
"perplexity < 500 on PTB-1k" and Stage 4 "≥60% vs the UD oracle" declarations were
retired after reachability review. Their restated gates are now recorded: Stage 3
uses the identical-position letter-bigram bar, and Stage 4 reports agreement on
named gold and derived populations. The underlying methodological lesson remains:
measure the trivial baseline and achievable ceiling *before* accepting a threshold.

## Local gotchas

**`build_letter_transition` builds the bigram transition, installed as `B`.**
`build_unigram_a` remains a compatibility alias to that same function object;
`build_letter_agent` retains the historical `a_matrix` keyword for existing
callers. Neither name licenses putting the learned matrix into `A`.

**`load_brown_counts` names the real generator.** Its `FileNotFoundError` points to
`scripts/generate_counts.py`, which regenerates the vendored Brown artifact.

**Float32 is JAX's default, so exact anchors land at ~2e-7 relative.** The
uniform-transition case lands a hair below 39.0 rather than exactly on it.
Compare with a tolerance, never for equality.

**`decode_positions` is slow by design.** One `infer_states` plus one
`update_empirical_prior` per position, in milliseconds per position — the full
test split (claim:stage2_g2p_test_pairs words) is minutes of live calls. That is
*why* the full-set statistics come from the numpy oracle after
`verify_decoder_equivalence` proves zero disagreements. If that equivalence ever
fails, the full-set numbers are void because they no longer describe the pymdp
agent.

**`build_g2p_transition` trains on the TRAIN split only.** It deliberately does not
reuse the artifact's full-corpus `phone_bigrams`, which include the held-out test
words. Substituting them leaks test statistics into the decoder.

**`load_phoneme_counts` validates the artifact's `phones` list against `PHONES`.**
That check exists because a reordered inventory would silently misindex every matrix
built from the file. Do not relax it to `try/except`.

**`full_corpus_perplexity` raises when `phone_initials` is absent** rather than
falling back to the length-matched subset. The fallback would flatter the model's
reported perplexity. Keep the raise.

## Verification for this directory

```bash
cd "$(git rev-parse --show-toplevel)"   # the repository root

# Unit + gate suites for both levels
.venv/bin/python -m pytest \
  tests/test_letter.py tests/test_phoneme.py tests/test_phoneme_full_corpus.py \
  tests/test_stage1_gate.py tests/test_stage2_gate.py -q --no-cov

# Are the thresholds still sound (baseline FAILS, ceiling CLEARS)?
.venv/bin/python scripts/audit_gates.py
```

`audit_gates.py` exits non-zero if any audited gate is not bracketed (VACUOUS, UNREACHABLE, or ABOVE_REFERENCE), or if
the model no longer sits on the right side of its bar. Its `--limit` bounds the G2P
subsample: enough to classify the gate, never enough to establish significance.
