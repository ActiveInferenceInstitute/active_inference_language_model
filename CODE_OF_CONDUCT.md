# Code of Conduct

This project adheres to the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
demonstrating empathy, being respectful of differing opinions and
experiences, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and
focusing on what is best for the overall community. Examples of unacceptable
behavior include harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and
publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

The full text — including enforcement responsibilities, scope, and
guidelines — is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>. That
text governs; this file is a summary, not a substitute.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to **daniel@activeinference.institute**. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and confidentiality will be
respected with regard to the reporter of any incident.
